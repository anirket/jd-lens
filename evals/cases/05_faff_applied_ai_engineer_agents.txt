Applied AI Engineer (Agents)
FAFF
Bengaluru, Karnataka | Full Time | On-Site, 6 days a week
Salary: 30-50 LPA

About FAFF
FAFF is a Bengaluru consumer-tech company building a human + AI personal assistant that people delegate everyday tasks to. They want engineers who go deep on problems; surface-level GenAI familiarity is not enough.

What you'll do
* Build and deploy AI agents and agentic workflows for real-world use cases
* Own AI problem statements end to end, from understanding the problem to production
* Design reliable systems combining LLMs, tools, retrieval, memory and structured workflows
* Experiment with different approaches and find what works best
* Improve accuracy, reliability, latency and cost of AI systems
* Build evaluation and monitoring for AI applications
* Work with product and engineering to turn real problems into AI solutions
* Debug failures and keep improving production AI systems

What we're looking for
* 2-3 years in AI/ML engineering or a closely related engineering role
* Current or previous experience at a product-based company
* Hands-on experience building and shipping AI/ML systems
* Experience building AI agents, agentic workflows or tool-calling systems
* Strong understanding of LLMs in production
* Strong programming skills, preferably Python
* Ability to take an ambiguous problem and go deep to solve it
* Experience with RAG, embeddings, vector databases, evaluation, prompt engineering or model integration
* Strong problem-solving and debugging skills
* Candidates with only surface-level GenAI/LLM exposure will not be a fit

Interview process
Intro screen, technical interview, AI problem-solving round, hiring manager round, founder round.

About Credo AI
Credo AI positions itself as the trust layer for enterprise AI governance. Its platform uses an agent-updated knowledge graph, enriched with regulatory intelligence and business context and backed by forward-deployed experts, to surface the risk behind each AI system.

About the role
Lead the AI Governance Agent initiative, cutting manual effort in AI risk management and compliance: risk identification, control selection, evidence evaluation, and eventually full governance workflows, with humans kept in the loop.

What you'll do
* Help build the Credo AI Agent, an assistant for governance workflows
* Combine rule-based systems with AI capabilities
* Build prototypes in the Labs environment
* Research alignment, evaluation and oversight methods
* Work with product, engineering and customer teams to take prototypes to production
* Lead thought leadership through publications and talks

Minimum qualifications
* Advanced degree or equivalent experience
* 3+ years building and deploying production LLM systems
* Prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation and LLM alignment
* Experience with multi-component agents or complex AI systems
* Proficiency in Python and modern AI tooling
* Understanding of AI risk, governance frameworks or compliance
* Strong communication skills

Preferred qualifications
* Experience with risk, compliance or governance systems
* Background in AI safety or responsible AI
* Track record of practical AI applications
* Rapid prototyping and experimental validation
* Publications in AI safety, LLMs or governance

Benefits
Base pay and equity, health/dental/vision, flexible remote work, unlimited PTO, professional development budget, ergonomic equipment benefit, coworking costs covered, up to 12 weeks paid parental leave.

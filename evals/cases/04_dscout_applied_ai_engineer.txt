Applied AI Engineer - India
Dscout
Remote - India | Full Time

About Dscout
Dscout runs a UX research platform used by large brands. The team builds AI-native product features, where success is measured by how well agents behave across real-world scenarios, not by passing a fixed test suite.

What you'll do
* Own the production improvement loop for AI features
* Instrument agent workflows so their behaviour is observable
* Build evaluation datasets and monitoring
* Diagnose underperformance across prompts, tools, routing and workflow design
* Build backend services and feedback pipelines
* Run A/B tests and staged rollouts
* Work with Product, Data Science and Sales

What we're looking for
* 2-5 years of software engineering experience
* LLM-powered features or agents shipped to production
* Prompt and context engineering
* Experience building evaluation harnesses
* Comfort working with non-deterministic systems
* Experiment design
* Daily use of AI coding tools

Nice to have
* Voice or real-time conversational AI
* LLM observability tools
* Agent orchestration frameworks such as LangGraph
* MCP-based tooling

Benefits
Bonus and equity, flexible PTO, 15 company holidays, 12 weeks paid parental leave, education stipend, remote work stipend.
